# Accessorial Charges Guide

Accessorial charges are additional fees applied on top of the base freight rate and fuel surcharge, for services beyond standard dock-to-dock delivery.

## Standard accessorial fees

| Service | Fee (flat, USD) | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Liftgate delivery | $75.00 | Delivery location has no loading dock and requires a hydraulic liftgate to unload |
| Residential delivery | $95.00 | Delivery address is a private residence, not a commercial address |
| Limited access delivery | $110.00 | Delivery location is a school, military base, construction site, or similarly restricted-access site |
| Inside delivery | $150.00 | Freight must be moved beyond the receiving dock into the building interior |
| Appointment/notification delivery | $50.00 | Receiver requires advance notice or a scheduled delivery window |

## Stacking rules

Accessorial fees are additive — a shipment requiring both liftgate and residential delivery is charged both fees in full, not a blended or discounted rate. There is no cap on the number of accessorial fees that can apply to a single shipment.

## Carrier variance

Not all carriers offer all accessorial services on every lane. Confirm accessorial availability with the specific carrier before quoting a rate that includes one.
