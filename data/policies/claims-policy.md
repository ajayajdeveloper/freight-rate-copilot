# Freight Damage Claims Policy

This policy covers the process and timelines for filing a claim when freight arrives damaged, short, or lost in transit.

## Filing window

Claims must be filed within **9 months** of the delivery date for concealed damage, and noted on the delivery receipt **at the time of delivery** for visible damage. Claims filed after the 9-month window will not be honored, regardless of the shipment's original rate agreement.

## Required documentation

A valid claim must include:
- The original bill of lading (BOL)
- Photographic evidence of the damage
- The delivery receipt, noted with damage if visible at time of delivery
- A completed claim form with the claimed dollar amount

## Claim value limits

Carrier liability for damaged or lost freight is limited to the **released value** declared on the bill of lading, not the full replacement cost of the goods, unless additional cargo insurance was purchased separately at time of booking.

## Processing time

Carriers are contractually obligated to respond to a properly filed claim within **30 business days**, either approving payment, denying with a documented reason, or requesting additional information. Claims left unresolved past 120 days should be escalated to the carrier's claims department supervisor.
