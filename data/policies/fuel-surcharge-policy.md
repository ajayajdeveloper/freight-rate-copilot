# Fuel Surcharge Policy

All LTL (less-than-truckload) rates quoted by our carrier network are subject to a fuel surcharge, calculated as a percentage of the base freight rate. The fuel surcharge percentage is set per carrier, per rate agreement, and is reviewed monthly based on the national average diesel price index.

## How the fuel surcharge is calculated

The total shipment cost is calculated as:

```
total_cost = base_rate + (base_rate * fuel_surcharge_pct / 100)
```

For example, a shipment with a base rate of $245.00 and a fuel surcharge of 18.5% would have a fuel surcharge amount of $45.33, for a total cost of $290.33.

## Important notes

- Fuel surcharge percentages differ by carrier and are locked in at the time a rate is quoted — they do not fluctuate mid-shipment once a rate has been issued.
- A quoted rate (base rate + fuel surcharge) is only valid between its effective date and its expiry date. Rates presented after their expiry date must be re-quoted, not honored at the old figure.
- Fuel surcharge percentages currently range from approximately 17% to 21% across our active carrier network, depending on carrier and lane.
