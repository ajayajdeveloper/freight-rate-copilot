# LTL Service Levels

Our carrier network offers three standard service levels for less-than-truckload (LTL) shipments. Service level affects transit time and price, but not the base rate calculation itself — accessorial and fuel surcharge rules apply identically across all service levels.

## Standard

Default service level for all quoted rates unless otherwise specified. Transit time is carrier-determined based on lane distance, typically 2-5 business days for most continental US lanes. No expedite premium applies.

## Expedited

Guarantees delivery within a shorter, carrier-specified window (typically 1-3 business days depending on lane). Expedited service carries a premium of **25% of the base rate**, applied before fuel surcharge and accessorial fees are calculated.

## Guaranteed / Time-Definite

Delivery is guaranteed by a specific date and time. If the carrier misses the guaranteed window, the shipment's base rate is refunded in full (fuel surcharge and accessorials are not refunded). Guaranteed service carries a premium of **40% of the base rate**, applied before fuel surcharge and accessorial fees are calculated.

## Choosing a service level

Service level does not change carrier eligibility, weight bracket rules, or rate expiry dates — those are governed entirely by the underlying rate agreement, independent of which service level is selected on top of it.
